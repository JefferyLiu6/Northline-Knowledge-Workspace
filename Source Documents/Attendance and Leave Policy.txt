Northline Industrial Systems Inc. Attendance and Leave Policy

Document Number: GL-HR-2024-006
Version: V2.3
Effective Date: July 1, 2024
Responsible Department: Human Resources Department

Chapter One: Attendance Regulations

Article 1: All employees must participate in attendance tracking. The attendance method is dual recording via the workforce portal location check-in and door access card swipe, with the workforce portal records as the primary reference, and door access records for anomaly verification.

Article 2: Two check-ins per day: one for morning arrival and one for evening departure. If the number of missed check-ins in a month does not exceed 3, the employee may submit a make-up check-in application via OA, which must be approved by the supervisor and will not be counted as an anomaly. If it exceeds 3, from the 4th missed check-in onwards, each instance will be treated as a late arrival.

Article 3: Tardiness and Early Departure:
Arriving at work within 30 minutes after the scheduled start time is considered tardy, and leaving before the scheduled end time by 30 minutes is considered early departure.
No deductions will be made if the total number of tardy or early departures in a month does not exceed 2 times.
Starting from the third occurrence, a deduction of 50 CAD will be made each time, and the performance evaluation will be downgraded by one level if the total exceeds 5 times in a month.

Article 4: Absence without prior approval:

If an employee arrives more than 30 minutes after the scheduled start time without prior approval, it will be treated as a half-day absence. If the employee does not show up for the entire day without proper leave procedures, it will be treated as a full-day absence.
Article 5: Absence Handling:
A full-day absence will result in a deduction of twice the daily wage.

If an employee is absent for 3 consecutive days or accumulates 5 days of absence in a year, the company may terminate the employment contract according to the Employee Handbook, Article 30.

Article 6: Out-of-office and business trip periods:

If an employee cannot clock in during out-of-office or business trip periods, they must submit an out-of-office or business trip application on OA, and the system will automatically exempt them from clocking in after approval. For employees stationed at customer sites, the project manager will uniformly confirm attendance in the weekly report.

Chapter 2: Types and Standards of Leave

Article 8: Annual Leave Entitled by Law:

An employee who has worked for a cumulative period of 1 to 10 years is entitled to 5 days of annual leave; 10 to 20 years, 10 days; and 20 years or more, 15 days. The cumulative work experience includes the time worked at other companies, and it is based on the social security payment records or the termination certificate.

Article 9: Company Benefits Leave:

An employee who has worked for at least 1 year in the company is entitled to an additional 2 days of benefits leave; 3 years, 4 days; and 5 years, 6 days. Benefits leave and statutory leave are combined for use, and it is valid for the current year, not carried over to the next year, and not converted into salary.

Article 10: Sick Leave:
Sick leave must be supported by a sick leave note and registration records from a hospital of level two or higher. If the total sick leave in a year does not exceed 10 days, it will be paid at 80% of the basic salary; for days exceeding 10, it will be paid at 80% of the local minimum wage standard.
If the sick leave exceeds 15 days consecutively, the employee must submit a return-to-work certificate before resuming work.

The medical leave is determined based on the length of service according to national regulations, and the company will not terminate the employment contract during the medical leave period.

Article 11: Personal Leave:
Personal leave is unpaid leave, and the salary will be deducted according to the daily wage standard. Daily wage = monthly wage ÷ 21.75.
For personal leave not exceeding 3 days, approval from the department head is required; for more than 3 days, approval from the vice president in charge is required; for more than 10 days, approval from the general manager is required.

If the total personal leave exceeds 20 days in a year, the employee will lose the eligibility for the annual bonus for that year.

Article 12: Marriage Leave:

An employee who is legally married is entitled to 13 days of marriage leave (including 3 days as stipulated by the state and 10 days as a local reward), including weekends and public holidays. The marriage leave must be taken within 12 months from the date of marriage registration, and it will expire if not taken within that period.

Article 13: Maternity Leave and Paternity Leave:
Female employees are entitled to 158 days of maternity leave, with 15 days available before childbirth. An additional 15 days will be granted for difficult childbirth, and an additional 15 days for each additional child.
Male employees are entitled to 15 days of paternity leave, which must be taken within 3 months from the date of their spouse's childbirth.

Female employees are entitled to 1 hour of breastfeeding time per day for the first year of their child's life, which can be combined as an hour earlier departure each day.

Article 14: Childcare Leave:

Both parents are entitled to 10 days of childcare leave per year before their child turns 3 years old. Childcare leave is paid based on attendance, and it is not carried over if not taken in the same year.

Article 15: Bereavement Leave:

Article 16 Employment injury leave

If an employee is recognized as having a work-related injury, their salary and welfare benefits during the period of suspended work shall remain unchanged and shall be paid by the company on a monthly basis. The period of suspended work generally does not exceed 12 months. For cases with severe injuries, the duration may be appropriately extended upon confirmation by the labor capacity appraisal committee.

Chapter Three - Leave Process

Article 17 - All types of leave must be applied for via the OA system. In case of emergency, employees may first notify their direct supervisor by phone, but must submit the application within 1 working day after returning to work. Failure to submit the application within the deadline will be treated as unauthorized absence.

Article 18 - Approval Authority:

Leave of 0.5 to 1 day         Approval by Direct Supervisor
Leave of more than 1 day to 3 days        Approval by Department Head
Leave of more than 3 days to 10 days       Approval by Deputy General Manager
Leave of more than 10 days             Approval by General Manager
Maternal Leave, Work Injury Leave               Approval by Department Head and then filing with the Human Resources Department

In principle, the annual leave must be applied for five working days in advance, avoiding peak production periods and key nodes of the project.

Article 20 - Work during the leave period must be assigned to a substitute and completed handover. The person on leave shall be held responsible if the work is delayed due to incomplete handover.

Chapter Four - Overtime and Compensatory Leave

Article 21 - Overtime is divided into three categories: overtime on workdays, overtime on rest days, and overtime on statutory holidays. Overtime applications must be submitted in advance via the OA system.

Article 22 - Overtime compensation methods:

Overtime on workdays       Converted to compensatory leave at 1.5 times, or paid at 1.5 times the regular rate
Overtime on rest days           Prioritizing compensatory leave of the same duration, or paid at 2 times the regular rate if compensatory leave is not possible
Overtime on statutory holidays       Paid at 3 times the regular rate, and compensatory leave cannot be used as a substitute

Article 23 - Compensatory leave must be used within 3 months from the date of overtime, or it will expire. The minimum unit of compensatory leave is 0.5 days.

Article 24 - For positions under the comprehensive working hour system, overtime is calculated based on the total working hours exceeding the statutory standard within the monthly cycle, not on a daily basis.

Chapter Five - Attendance Statistics and Appeals

Article 25 - The Human Resources Department generates the attendance report for the previous month by the 3rd of each month and sends it to the department heads for confirmation.

Article 26 - If employees have objections to the attendance records, they must submit an appeal to the Human Resources Department within 2 working days after receiving the report, and provide supporting materials such as access records, work emails, and system operation logs. Failure to appeal within the deadline is considered as acceptance.

Article 27 - Attendance results are one of the bases for performance evaluation, year-end bonus distribution, and commendation and excellence awards.

Chapter Six - Supplementary Provisions

Article 28 - This regulation is interpreted by the Human Resources Department. In case of inconsistency with the Employee Handbook, this regulation shall prevail.

Article 29 - This regulation shall take effect from the date of its release, and the original "Attendance Management Regulations" (GL-HR-2022-006) shall be simultaneously abolished.
