# Northline Industrial Systems Inc. Employee Handbook

Document Number: GL-HR-2024-001
Version: V4.1
Effective Date: July 1, 2024
Interpretation Department: Human Resources Department

## Chapter One General Provisions

Article 1: To standardize company management and clarify employees' rights and obligations, this handbook is formulated based on the "company employment policy," the "individual employment agreements," and other relevant laws and regulations, combined with the company's actual situation.

Article 2: This handbook applies to all employees who have signed labor contracts with the company, including regular employees and those in the probationary period. Interns and dispatched workers are subject to this handbook, unless otherwise agreed.

Article 3: Employees must carefully read this handbook and sign a confirmation upon joining. This handbook takes effect from the date of implementation, and any previous regulations inconsistent with this handbook will be replaced by this handbook.

## Chapter 2: Onboarding and Probationary Period

Article 4: New employees must submit the following documents upon reporting for duty: original and copies of ID card, highest academic degree and diploma, proof of termination of previous employment, health examination report, copies of bank cards, and two one-inch photos. Academic information will be verified by the Human Resources Department through the approved credential-verification provider.

Article 5: If false documents are provided, the company has the right to terminate the labor contract without paying economic compensation. If discovered after employment, it will be treated as a serious disciplinary offense.

Article 6: The probation period is determined based on the term of the labor contract:

- If the labor contract term is more than 1 year but less than 3 years, the probation period is 2 months
- If the labor contract term is more than 3 years, the probation period is 6 months
- If the labor contract term is more than 3 months but less than 1 year, the probation period is 1 month

Article 7: The probationary salary is 80% of the regular salary, and shall not be lower than the minimum wage standard in the company's location. For R&D positions, the probationary salary is 90% of the regular salary.

Article 8: 15 working days before the expiration of the probationary period, staff submit a request for conversion, the assessment is completed by their immediate supervisor and approved by the head of department.

Article 9: Employees on probation must give 3 days' written notice before leaving; formal employees must give 30 days' written notice before leaving.

## Chapter Three Working Hours

Article 10: The company implements a standard working hours system, with 8 hours of work per day and 5 days of work per week. The working hours are from 9:00 to 18:00, with 12:00 to 13:00 as the lunch break.

Article 11: Production departments implement a comprehensive working hours system, with a monthly cycle and total monthly working hours not exceeding the statutory standard. Specific shift arrangements are formulated by the Production Department and reported to the Human Resources Department for record.

Flexible work: Research and development, design and product jobs can apply for flexible working hours ranging from 8:30 to 10:00.

Article 13: Overtime must be applied for in advance through the OA system and approved by the direct supervisor to be valid. Voluntary extended work without approval does not count as overtime. Overtime can be compensated by either taking compensatory leave or receiving overtime pay, with compensatory leave valid for 3 months from the date of overtime.

## Chapter Four Salary and Benefits

Article 14: The salary structure is: monthly total income = basic salary + position salary + performance salary + allowances. Basic salary and position salary are fixed, while performance salary fluctuates based on quarterly performance evaluations.

Article 15: Salary is paid on the 15th of each month, and if it falls on a holiday, it will be paid on the nearest working day. Salaries are transferred via bank, and personal income tax is withheld and paid by the company.

Article 16: The company provides the benefits described in each employee's agreement. Human Resources explains health coverage, retirement savings options, and payroll deductions during onboarding.

Article 17: Allowance items:

- Lunch allowance: 30 CAD per working day, paid together with salary
- Transportation allowance: 200 CAD per month, 500 CAD per month for fieldwork positions
- Communication allowance: 100 CAD per month, 300 CAD per month for sales and after-sales positions
- High-temperature allowance: 300 CAD per month from June to September
- On-site allowance: 150 CAD per day for employees who stay on-site at the client's location for at least 5 consecutive working days

Article 18: Annual benefits include: one annual health check-up, 500 CAD birthday gift, 1000 CAD wedding gift, 1000 CAD maternity gift, and festival gifts for the year-end holiday and the company anniversary. Employees with 3 years of service receive an upgraded comprehensive health check-up package.

Article 19: Annual bonus is linked to the company's annual business performance and individual performance, generally paid in February of the following year. Employees who have left before the payment date are not eligible for the bonus.

## Chapter Five Performance Management

Article 20: The company implements quarterly performance evaluations, with evaluation periods from January to March, April to June, July to September, and October to December each year.

Article 21: The evaluation grades are divided into S, A, B, C, and D, corresponding to performance coefficients of 1.5, 1.2, 1.0, 0.8, and 0. The proportion of S grades within a department shall not exceed 10%, and A grades shall not exceed 30%.

Article 22 Employees who receive a C rating for two consecutive quarters shall enter a 3-month performance improvement plan. If they fail to meet the standards by the end of the improvement period, the company may reassign them or terminate their employment in accordance with the law.

Article 23 Employees who receive a D rating in a single quarter shall receive zero performance-based salary for that quarter and shall undergo a face-to-face discussion with their department head and the Human Resources Department.

## Chapter Six Training and Development

Article 24 New employees shall attend a 3-day onboarding training covering company overview, product knowledge, rules and regulations, and information security. Those who fail the training may take one make-up exam.

Article 25 Technical positions shall receive no less than 40 hours of professional training annually. For external training organized by the company, if the single session cost exceeds 5,000 CAD, the employee must sign a training service agreement with a service period of 2 years. If the employee resigns voluntarily during the service period, the company shall refund the training fees proportionally based on the number of unfulfilled months.

Article 26 Employees may apply for internal job transfers. Those who have been employed for at least one year and have a performance rating of B or higher are eligible to apply. The transfer must be approved by both the original and receiving departments, and the employee will have a 1-month adaptation period after the transfer.

## Chapter Seven Code of Conduct

Article 27 Employees shall abide by professional ethics and shall not engage in part-time work that competes with the company's business or use their position for personal gain.

Article 28 Employees shall not accept cash, securities, or valuable items from suppliers or customers. Gifts received for business purposes with a value exceeding 500 CAD shall be reported to the Administration Department for registration and handling.

Article 29 Employees in confidential positions must complete the handover of confidential matters upon leaving and comply with the terms of the non-compete agreement. The maximum non-compete period shall not exceed 2 years, and the company shall pay monthly compensation.

Article 30 Serious disciplinary violations include, but are not limited to: leaking company trade secrets, forging reimbursement documents, continuous absence for 3 days or accumulated absence for 5 days in a year, fighting in the workplace, and serious negligence causing significant economic loss. In such cases, the company may immediately terminate the employment contract.

## Chapter Eight Employee Departure Management

Article 31 Employees must complete work handover, including pending matters, documents, office equipment, access cards, and account permissions. After the handover is completed and both parties sign to confirm, the Human Resources Department will process the departure procedures.

Article 32 The company shall issue a departure certificate upon termination or termination of the labor contract and shall complete the transfer of employee records and benefits records within 15 days.

Article 33 The salary for the month of departure shall be paid together with the regular salary on the next normal payroll date. Unused statutory annual leave shall be paid at 300% of the daily wage.

## Chapter Nine Supplementary Provisions

Article 34 This manual is interpreted and revised by the Human Resources Department. The revised version shall be released after being approved by the General Manager's Office Meeting and shall take effect from the date of release.

Article 35 Matters not covered in this manual shall be governed by national laws and regulations and the company's other policies.
